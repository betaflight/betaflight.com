# Welcome to the Manufacturer Section

### Information for Manufacturers

Betaflight is free, open-source flight control software for every kind of drone, and runs on hardware from many manufacturers. We work with those manufacturers so that they, and the pilots who fly their hardware, get the best out of Betaflight.

Start with the [Hardware Policy](hardware-policy.md). It sets out what we prefer, support, permit and warn against, and who supports a board once it ships. In short: we fix Betaflight, manufacturers support their hardware, and pilots own their builds.

This section also covers the [Manufacturer Design Guidelines](manufacturer-design-guidelines.md), the [Connector Standard](connector-standard.mdx), [creating](creating-configuration.md) and [submitting](requirements-for-submission-of-targets.md) a board config, and the [Betaflight Supported](betaflight-supported.mdx) and [Betaflight Partners](partner-program.mdx) programmes. Contact details are given throughout, and we are happy to work through a translation bot so that we can collaborate easily.
