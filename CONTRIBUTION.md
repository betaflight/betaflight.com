## Licence and Contributor Licence Agreement

The content of betaflight.com is not open source. All rights are reserved, except for the content listed in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

When you open a pull request, you agree to the [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md) for all work in it. If your pull request includes work by other people, you confirm that you have the right to submit it. If you use AI or other automated tools, you take responsibility for the result as your own contribution. You keep the copyright in your contribution and can use it for any purpose. Betaflight gets a licence to publish it.

Do not copy content from other websites or documents unless you have the right to do so. Give the source and its licence in your pull request.

## Docs

For writing docs you should the MDX reference on the docusaurus page [MDX Reference](https://docusaurus.io/docs/markdown-features/react).
This is a really powerful feature!

You can look in the current docs for examples to get a idea of what can be done.

## Blog

For blog posts, the same MDX features apply.
To get your name and some header display on the post i refere to [here](https://docusaurus.io/docs/blog#adding-posts).

Please add yourself to the `blog\authors.yaml` file.
