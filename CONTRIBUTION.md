# Contribution Guide

## Licence and Contributor Licence Agreement

The content of betaflight.com is not open source. All rights are reserved, except for the content listed in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

When you open a pull request, you agree to the [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md). Tick the agreement box in the pull request description to confirm. If other people author or commit work in your pull request, each of them must also add a comment that says only "I agree to the Contributor Licence Agreement". If a commit or co-author is not linked to a GitHub account, a maintainer must verify that contributor. A check on your pull request fails until all of this is done. If you use AI or other automated tools, you take responsibility for the result as your own contribution. You keep the copyright in your contribution and can use it for any purpose. Betaflight gets a licence to publish it.

Do not copy content from other websites or documents unless you have the right to do so. Give the source and its licence in your pull request.
